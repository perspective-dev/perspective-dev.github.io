Raycasting

Heatmap chart of color, grouped by x, split by y, with 3 computed expression columns.

A raytraced torus, rendered by an ExprTK expression.

Data

A raytraced torus rendered entirely by Perspective's columnar expression language over a generated pixel grid, as a heatmap.

Generated in the browser when the example loads.

About Perspective

Perspective is an open-source, WebAssembly-powered data grid, pivot table and charting component for large, real-time and streaming datasets — in the browser, Python, Jupyter, Node.js and Rust.
